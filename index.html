Gwella is Cornish for “better” or “best”.

Gwella Mentoring helps young people move towards their “better” — with clearer direction, stronger confidence and practical next steps.

Is your teenager struggling with confidence, motivation or direction?

Does your teenager seem stuck?

Sometimes young people don't need another person telling them what to do. They need someone outside home and school who will listen, ask the right questions, challenge their thinking and help them work out their own next step.

Gwella provides a calm, supportive space where young people can talk things through, gain perspective and begin moving forward.

About Phil and Gwella

Gwella Mentoring was founded by Phil Petchey following a career spanning more than 30 years working with young people in education.

Over that time Phil has developed a range of practical tools and approaches designed to help young people understand themselves, build confidence, strengthen resilience and make positive decisions.

Sessions are friendly, down-to-earth and focused on small, manageable changes that build over time.

Away from mentoring, Phil is a sailor, surfer, runner and questionable ukulele player.

How Gwella Can Help

For Parents

Gwella can support young people who are:

  • struggling with confidence or self-belief
  • lacking motivation
  • feeling stuck or unsure what to do next
  • finding school or life overwhelming
  • capable but not fulfilling their potential
  • trying to build resilience in sport or everyday life
  • facing important choices or transitions

For Schools & Professionals

Gwella provides an additional supportive relationship for young people who may benefit from focused one-to-one mentoring alongside the support already available through school, college or other services.

Mentoring can focus on confidence, motivation, aspirations, resilience, goal setting, decision-making and preparation for future transitions.

Gwella Next

For young people aged 16–20 who are figuring out what's next.

Moving beyond school can bring exciting opportunities but also uncertainty. Gwella Next provides a space for older teenagers and young adults to think clearly about where they are heading and what practical steps they can take.

  • Leaving school
  • College or university decisions
  • Careers
  • Apprenticeships
  • Finding motivation
  • Building confidence
  • First jobs
  • Changing direction
  • Setting goals
  • Life after school
